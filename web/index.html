Lyrics, Scripture and media on screen for Sunday service.

NLPresenter runs the screens for your worship service from one computer. You plan the service, then click through songs, Bible verses, videos and announcements while the projector, the stage screen and the livestream follow along.

What it does

Everything a church media team needs on a Sunday, in one window. Here are the main parts.

Plan the service, then run it

Build the order of service from songs, Bible passages, pictures, videos, presentations and text slides. Group items under headers like “Worship” or “The Word” and give each one a planned length.

On Sunday, press → or click a slide. The live preview, up-next slide and timers keep you on track.

A song library with chords

Type or paste lyrics, mark verses and choruses, and add chords for the band. Change the key with one click. Arrange the sections differently for each service.

Can't find a song? Search for its lyrics online, or import ChordPro and SongSelect files.

Bible verses in seconds

Type jn 3:16-18 or a few words you remember. Pick the verses and show them live. Compare up to three translations side by side.

KJV, ASV and a Tagalog Bible come built in. Install more than 1,000 free translations from the library, or import your own.

Stage screens and livestream

Singers and musicians get their own screen with big lyrics, chords, the next line and a clock. Send them messages like “Repeat chorus” during the song.

A transparent lyrics overlay drops straight into OBS or vMix for your livestream.

Control it from your phone

Scan a QR code and your phone becomes a remote: next, previous, black screen, jump to any item, or search a Bible verse. The pastor can chat with the operator from the stage.

Tablets and TVs on the same Wi-Fi can show the stage view or mirror the projector, with no app to install.

Your first service in five steps

The quick start walks through each step in detail.

  1. Install and open NLPresenter.

    Connect the projector or TV as a second display.

  2. Add songs.

    Songs tab → New song, or Find a song to search online.

  3. Build the service.

    Double-click songs and Bible passages to add them to the service order.

  4. Pick a background and rehearse.

    Choose a background, then click Rehearse to try it without anyone seeing.

  5. Go live.

    Click Open Output, then press → or Space to move through the slides.
